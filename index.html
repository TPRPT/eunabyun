Knight Path: Supporting Player Sense-making in Game Narratives via Visual Scaffolding and Spoiler-Aware AI

Euna Byun, Jae-Yeop Jeong, Yewon Min, Jin-Woo Jeong

CHI 2026 Extended Abstracts · Poster

Progress-synchronized visual scaffolding and spoiler-aware AI support for making sense of game narratives.게임 진행 상황에 연동되는 시각적 구조화와 스포일러를 고려한 AI 지원을 통해 게임 서사 이해를 돕는 시스템.

Paper / ACM DL ↗논문 / ACM DL ↗
Abstract초록 (영문 원문)

Fragmented narrative delivery in games often causes cognitive gaps, while external resources risk spoilers and information overload. We present Knight Path, an adaptive system integrating structural visual scaffolding with on-demand, spoiler-aware AI guidance. The system combines interactive narrative visualization with a tiered AI assistant synchronized with live gameplay. A user study (N = 18) in a non-linear environment showed significant improvements in search efficiency and contextual clarity while preserving narrative immersion. Knight Path effectively addresses the conflict between seeking assistance and sustaining the joy of discovery by empowering players to manage narrative uncertainty.

Investigating the Impact of Lying Postures on Mixed Reality Interaction

Yujin Jeong*, Jiwon Lee*, Jae-Yeop Jeong, Euna Byun, HyeokJu Ko, Jin-Woo Jeong

CHI 2026 Extended Abstracts · Poster

Examining how lying postures affect interaction performance in mixed reality.누운 자세가 혼합현실 환경의 상호작용 수행에 미치는 영향을 분석한 연구.

* Equal contribution* 공동 제1저자

Paper / ACM DL ↗논문 / ACM DL ↗
Abstract초록 (영문 원문)

As MR head-mounted displays integrate into daily life, users increasingly interact while reclining, yet how lying positions affect interaction performance and task load remains underexplored. We conducted a user study (N=18) evaluating 3D manipulation and pointing tasks across supine, left-lateral, and right-lateral postures. Results reveal significant differences in performance and comfort across lying positions, demonstrating the necessity of posture-adaptive interfaces for consistent usability in recumbent MR interaction.
